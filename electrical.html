EMMIMEH NIG. LTD.

Electrical Installation you can plan around.

Electrical installations are planned around project specifications, the intended use of the facility and applicable safety requirements. We focus on organised workmanship, appropriate materials and clear communication.

Our services include

  • Electrical wiring
  • Power distribution and distribution board installation
  • Internal and external lighting
  • Cable laying, termination and containment
  • Switches, sockets and electrical accessories
  • Circuit breakers and protective devices
  • Cable tray, trunking and conduit installation
  • Earthing and bonding installations
  • Control panel and electrical equipment installation
  • Fault identification, troubleshooting and maintenance
  • Testing and commissioning where included in the project scope

Final scope, specifications, deliverables and timelines are confirmed with the client before work begins.
