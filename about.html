OUR COMPANY

Practical solutions.
Professional delivery.

KATAH NIG. LTD. is an engineering services and technical supply company focused on electrical installations, mechanical installations, plumbing installations, M&E services drawings and the supply of electrical, mechanical and plumbing materials.

We support clients throughout different stages of their projects, from technical drawing preparation and installation planning to engineering works and the supply of required materials.

We understand that successful projects require coordination, attention to specifications, effective communication and dependable service. Our aim is to provide practical solutions that respond to each client's technical requirements, budget and project objectives.
