EMMIMEH NIG. LTD.

Plumbing systems that work reliably.

We provide plumbing installation services for residential buildings, commercial properties and industrial facilities. Our work supports reliable water supply, effective drainage and properly functioning sanitary systems.

Our services include

  • Cold-water supply pipe installation
  • Hot-water distribution pipework
  • Plumbing pipework and fittings installation
  • Drainage and wastewater systems
  • Sanitary ware installation
  • Water closets, wash-hand basins, sinks and related fixtures
  • Water storage tank connections and distribution
  • Pumps, valves and plumbing accessories installation
  • Plumbing maintenance and repairs
  • Leak detection and plumbing fault rectification
  • Plumbing system testing and inspection
  • Plumbing installation for new buildings and renovations

Final scope, specifications, deliverables and timelines are confirmed with the client before work begins.
